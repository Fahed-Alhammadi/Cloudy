• Changing your password now signs out your other devices too; this iPhone stays signed in (needs server 2.1.3).
• The welcome screen is gone: Cloudy opens straight on sign-in.
• iOS now asks for each permission only when you use the feature: local network when you sign in to a server at a local address, camera when you scan a document, photos when you save a picture, Face ID when you turn on App lock.
• If you decline local network access, sign-in explains why it can't reach your server and offers a button to open Settings.
