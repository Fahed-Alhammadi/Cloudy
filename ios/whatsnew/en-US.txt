• Cloudy in the Files app: your Cloudy files now appear under Locations in Apple's Files app, and in the open and save screens of other apps. Open, save, rename, move and delete files there, with picture previews. The first time, the Files app asks you to turn Cloudy on.
• Files shared with you are in the "Shared with you" folder.
• Signing out removes Cloudy from the Files app. With App lock on, Cloudy stays out of the Files app, so your files are only reachable with Face ID.
